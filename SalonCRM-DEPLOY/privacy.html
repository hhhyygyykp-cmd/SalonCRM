Privacy Policy

Belle Beauty Lounge · Al Ain, UAE · Last updated: August 2026

Belle Beauty Lounge (“we”, “us”) operates a staff CRM and WhatsApp messaging tools for salon customer care. This policy explains what information we process and why.

Information we collect

  • Customer name, phone / WhatsApp number, optional email, location/area, visit and service history, and loyalty tier.
  • Staff account details used to sign in to the CRM (name, email, role).
  • Message content and delivery status when we send WhatsApp notifications on your behalf.

How we use information

  • To manage appointments, visits, loyalty rewards, and salon operations.
  • To send WhatsApp messages such as thank-you notes, reminders, and service updates.
  • To operate loyalty / wallet features linked to a customer profile.

WhatsApp / Meta

When you message or receive messages from our business WhatsApp number, Meta processes those messages under Meta’s terms. We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) only to contact customers who have a relationship with Belle Beauty Lounge (for example after a visit or when they request contact).

Sharing

We do not sell customer data. We use service providers such as Supabase (database/auth) and Meta (WhatsApp) to run the CRM. Access inside the salon is limited to authorized staff.

Retention

We keep customer and visit records while needed for salon operations and legal requirements. You may ask us to update or delete your personal data where applicable.
